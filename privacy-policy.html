Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Steal My Agency ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit this website and submit your information through our registration form.

By submitting the form on this site, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit the registration form on this website, we collect:

  • First and last name as entered in the form
  • Email address that you provide
  • Mobile phone number that you provide
  • Consent record including the date, time, IP address, and the exact consent wording shown to you at the moment you submitted the form

We may also collect technical data such as browser type, device information, and the pages you visited. We do not collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or health information through this form.

3. SMS Program: Data Collection

When you tick the SMS consent box on this website, we collect and store:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • The date and time you submitted the opt-in form
  • The exact wording of the consent you agreed to
  • The IP address the consent was submitted from

We keep this record so we can show, on request, that you gave consent before we sent you any message.

4. SMS Program: How We Use Your Mobile Number

Your mobile phone number is used only to send you messages related to the Steal My Agency Training Alerts program. Message frequency varies, up to 6 messages per month.

We use your mobile number to:

  • Send you reminders before a training you registered for begins
  • Send you the replay link after the training
  • Follow up about the training you registered for

We will never use your mobile number to send you unrelated marketing, spam, or promotional messages from third parties.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Mobile Number

We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your mobile phone number with any third party for their own marketing purposes. Your mobile number is used only inside our own organization and by the CRM and messaging platform we use to deliver the messages you asked for.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party.

6. SMS Opt-Out and Help

To stop the messages: reply STOP to any text message you get from us. You will get one final confirmation message and then no further messages.

For help: reply HELP to any message, or email nathan@stealmyagency.com.

You can also opt out by emailing nathan@stealmyagency.com with "SMS Opt-Out" in the subject line. Opting out of SMS does not remove you from our email list, and unsubscribing from email does not stop the SMS messages. Use both if you want to stop everything.

7. SMS Platform and Carrier Liability

We send SMS messages through GoHighLevel, our CRM and messaging platform. Your mobile phone number and your opt-in consent record are stored in GoHighLevel's systems. See the GoHighLevel Privacy Policy.

Carrier liability: carriers such as AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, and others are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Message and data rates may apply. Standard rates from your wireless carrier apply to every message sent and received.

8. How We Use Your Other Information

We use the information you give us to:

  • Send you the training link and the materials you registered for
  • Send you reminders and the replay by email
  • Follow up about the training and let you know about related programs we run
  • Improve this website and measure how well our advertising works
  • Meet our legal obligations and protect against fraud

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

9. Third-Party Tools

To run this website and manage your registration we use:

  • GoHighLevel, our CRM and messaging platform, which stores your contact details and delivers our email and SMS. See the GoHighLevel Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website and processes the form submission. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Each provider has its own privacy policy covering how it handles data. We encourage you to read them.

10. Cookies and Tracking

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working and to measure how it performs. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Turning cookies off does not stop you from registering.

11. How We Protect Your Data

We use security measures including encrypted connections and access-controlled storage to protect your personal information. No transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please be careful about what you share online.

12. Data Retention

We keep your contact information in our CRM for as long as we need it to follow up with you and manage our relationship. SMS opt-in and opt-out records are kept for as long as telecommunications regulations require, even after you opt out, because we have to be able to prove the consent existed and that you revoked it.

If you ask us to delete your information, we will do so within a reasonable time unless we are required to keep it by law.
